Strategy Rules & Independent 500-Tick Scanner

Over / Under

On 1,000 ticks, Over N candidates require digits 0…N each below 10%. Under N candidates require digits N…9 each below 10%.

Differs

Use the most frequent digit in 1,000 ticks as barrier; random tie-break. Check payout and risk first.

Even / Odd

Compare parity percentages. Signal only if the difference is at least 5 percentage points; propose the less frequent parity.

Matches / Equals

Use the most frequent digit; require at least 15% frequency and acceptable quote/payout.

Hot / Cold

Hot ≥13%, cold ≤8%; signal only if the same classification persists across two rolling windows.
